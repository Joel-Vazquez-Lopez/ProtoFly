# ProtoFly

**From connectome to communication.**

ProtoFly explores whether populations of connectome-constrained digital
*Drosophila* can develop structured communication through non-linguistic
interaction alone.

## Core question

Can communication emerge in artificial agents whose neural architecture is
constrained by the biological connectome of the fruit fly, without providing
predefined words, symbols, syntax, or linguistic representations?

## Principle

ProtoFly begins with **no language**.

The agents are not given:

- words or tokens
- semantic labels
- predefined signals
- grammar
- language-specific objectives

Any communicative convention must emerge through interaction, learning, and
the need to coordinate behaviour.

## Roadmap

1. Reconstruct a connectome-constrained digital Drosophila.
2. Validate neural and sensory dynamics.
3. Introduce biologically plausible learning.
4. Validate learning in a single digital fly.
5. Create a population of five independently learning flies.
6. Introduce tasks requiring information transfer.
7. Measure the emergence of shared signalling conventions.
8. Test compositionality, productivity, and other language-like properties.
9. Identify the neural mechanisms and architectural limits underlying these transitions.

## Status

ProtoFly is currently in **Phase 0: biological and computational foundations**.
